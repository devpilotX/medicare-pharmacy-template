FAQ

Delivery questions

How fast is delivery really?

Most orders inside 5 km reach in 25–35 minutes. Late nights may take a little longer.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum. Even a single strip of paracetamol gets free delivery within 5 km.

What if I'm not home?

The rider will call you. We can also hand it to a neighbour, guard, or family member if you tell us in advance.

Can I cancel after ordering?

Yes, until the rider has left the store. Just WhatsApp us “cancel”.
