FAQ

Common questions

Is my prescription kept private?

Yes. Only our licensed pharmacist sees it. We don't share it with any third party.

What if a medicine is out of stock?

We'll suggest a substitute of the same composition (with your approval), or refund the amount.

Do I need a prescription for every medicine?

Only for schedule-H drugs (antibiotics, painkillers above OTC limits, etc.). General items don't need one.

Can I send a hand-written note instead?

For OTC items — yes. For prescription medicines, we need a valid doctor's prescription.
